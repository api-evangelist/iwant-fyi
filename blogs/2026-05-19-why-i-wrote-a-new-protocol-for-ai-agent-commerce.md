Launch announcement for the iwant.fyi demand-side protocol v1.0. The agent ecosystem has standardized how agents complete a purchase but not how they find what to purchase. The iwant.fyi demand-side protocol fills that gap with an open spec, MCP-native, Apache 2.0.
