# Macaroon Network

> Predicate-gated APIs that autonomous agents can discover and purchase.

## Machine discovery

- OpenAPI: https://api.macaroonnetwork.com/openapi.json
- AI catalog: https://api.macaroonnetwork.com/.well-known/ai-catalog.json
- A2A agent card: https://api.macaroonnetwork.com/.well-known/agent-card.json
- MCP endpoint: https://api.macaroonnetwork.com/mcp
- x402 discovery: https://api.macaroonnetwork.com/.well-known/x402

## Christian evidence APIs

Source-labelled BSB/WEB scripture evidence and bounded devotional products are available under the christian-* capability IDs. Runtime requests do not call third-party scripture providers.
